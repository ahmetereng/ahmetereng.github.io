Privacy Policy

Chef Fresh · Effective date: August 23, 2026

Chef Fresh ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is a kitchen-inventory app that helps you track groceries, get expiry reminders, and reduce food waste. We built it to collect as little as possible: there are no ads, no third-party tracking, and we do not sell your data. This policy explains what we handle, why, the legal grounds we rely on, how long we keep it, and the rights you have.

The short version. Your kitchen lives on your device. If you sign in, it also syncs to our cloud so it follows you across devices. AI features (scanning and recipe suggestions) send your input to Google's Gemini API — the app explains exactly what is sent and asks you to accept before anything leaves your device. Payments go through Apple or Google — we never see your card. You can delete your account and all synced data at any time from Settings.

1. Who we are (data controller)

Chef Fresh is developed and operated by Ahmet Eren, an independent developer based in the Republic of Türkiye ("we", the "data controller"). We decide how and why your personal data is processed in the app. For any privacy question or request, contact aegerapps@gmail.com (see Contact).

2. Data the app handles

  • Account. Signing in with Google or Apple gives us your name, email address, and a unique account ID. If you continue as a guest, only an anonymous random ID is created — no name, no email.
  • Kitchen data. Your fridge items, shopping list, kitchen membership, and chosen display name. This lives on your device and, when you are signed in, is synced to our cloud database so your kitchen follows you across devices. Guest data stays on the device only. Your consumed/wasted history (the Recap feature) is stored on your device only and is never uploaded.
  • AI input. What you submit to an AI feature: the photo you take or pick, the voice note you record, the text you type, and — when you ask for recipe suggestions — the names of the items currently in your fridge. Your app language is sent along so answers come back in it. This is collected only at the moment you start an AI action (camera, microphone, keyboard, or your existing fridge list), never in the background. It is sent over an encrypted connection through our server to Google's Gemini API, used once to answer that request, and not stored by us afterwards. See AI features for the full detail. Please don't include personal information in what you send.
  • Subscription status. Payments happen entirely in the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat links your subscription status to your account ID so Premium works across your devices. We never see or store your payment card or bank details.
  • Technical data. A device identifier tracks the free-scan trial: on iOS a random ID stored in the device Keychain, on Android the system-provided app-scoped device ID (ANDROID_ID). It is tied to the device rather than the app installation, so it persists if you delete and reinstall the app — this is what keeps the free trial from being reset by reinstalling. Our servers also use standard rate limiting that briefly processes IP addresses. Both are used purely to prevent abuse; we do not build advertising or behavioural profiles.

5. AI features: what is shared with Google

Chef Fresh has no AI model of its own. Two features — scanning (recognising groceries from a photo, a voice note, or typed text) and recipe suggestions — work by sending your input to Google's Gemini API and showing you what comes back. Because that means your data leaves your device and reaches a third-party AI service, we set it out in full here.

What is sent

  • The photo you take or choose from your library, when you scan.
  • The voice note you record, when you speak your groceries.
  • The text you type into the AI message box.
  • For recipe suggestions: the names of the items currently in your fridge, plus any note and preferences you set on that screen.
  • Your app language, so the answer comes back in the language you read.

What is never sent

  • Your name, e-mail address, or account ID.
  • Your location — the app never asks for it.
  • Your Recap history, shopping list, or kitchen-membership details.

Who receives it, and what they may do with it

The recipient is Google, through the Gemini API. The request travels from your device to our own server (Supabase Edge Functions) over an encrypted connection and is forwarded from there — the API key never sits on your device. We use the paid tier of the Gemini API, under which Google's own terms state that it does not use your prompts or the responses to train or improve its models, and there is no human review for model improvement. Google logs the request for a limited period solely to detect abuse of its service. Google's handling is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which commit it to protections equivalent to those described in this policy.

Your permission

Before anything is sent to Google, and before you can use the app, Chef Fresh shows you a screen naming Google and Gemini, listing exactly what will be transmitted, and asking you to accept. Nothing is sent unless you tap Accept and continue.

AI recognition and AI recipes are what Chef Fresh is — the app is built around them rather than offering them as an extra — so accepting is required to use it. If you do not want your input shared with Google on these terms, please do not accept: simply close the app, and nothing will have been sent. You can withdraw at any later point by deleting your account in Settings → Account and removing the app, which stops any further sharing and erases the data we hold (see Deleting your data). Because nothing you send is retained after a request completes, there is nothing left with Google to delete.

How long it is kept

We do not store your AI input or the response: both exist only for the seconds the request takes. Recognised items are saved only after you review and confirm them, and are then ordinary fridge data.

A note on accuracy

AI output is a suggestion, not a fact. Item names, quantities, estimated expiry dates and recipes should be checked before you rely on them — especially for allergies and food safety.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Account and synced kitchen data — kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed from our active systems promptly and purged from encrypted backups within 30 days.
  • AI input (scan content and recipe requests) — not retained after the request completes (a matter of seconds); it is processed in transit and discarded. Google keeps a short abuse-prevention log of its own, as described in section 5.
  • IP addresses — processed transiently for rate limiting and not stored as persistent logs beyond the short abuse-prevention window.
  • Device free-scan identifier — kept to enforce the trial. Because it lives in the iOS Keychain or is Android's system device ID, it persists on your device after you delete the app, and our record of used free scans may be retained after app or account deletion so the free trial cannot be reset by reinstalling. It identifies only the device's trial usage and is not linked to advertising or profiling.
  • On-device data (including Recap history) — stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Support correspondence — kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.

9. Shared kitchens

If you share a kitchen, its members see the shared inventory and the display name you chose — that's the point of the feature. Any member can edit the shared items. Leaving a kitchen keeps a private local copy of the items on your own device. Only invite people you trust.

10. Device permissions & notifications

The app asks for these device permissions only when you use the related feature, and you can revoke any of them in your device settings. Separately from these, the app asks you to accept the sharing of AI input with Google before you can use it at all — see section 5.

  • Camera — to photograph groceries for AI scanning.
  • Microphone — to let you say what you bought (voice scanning).
  • Photos — to pick an existing grocery image to scan.
  • Notifications — expiry and recap reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device. Turn them off anytime in the app's Settings or your device's system settings.

11. Your rights

Subject to the laws that apply to you (including the GDPR/UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — have your data deleted ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing relies on consent, at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection supervisory authority. In Türkiye this is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK); in the EU, your national authority.

You can exercise most of these directly in the app (see Deleting your data) or by emailing us. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally one month under the GDPR) and will not charge you or discriminate against you for exercising a right.

13. Deleting your data

You are in control:

  • Delete Account (Settings → Account) permanently deletes your account and the data synced to our servers. This cannot be undone.
  • Signing out removes the synced data from that device.
  • Deleting the app removes all on-device data, including Recap history.

For any other privacy request, contact us at the address below and we'll help as required by the laws that apply to you.

14. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS), and cloud data is protected with row-level security so each account can only reach its own kitchen's data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information and keep our data footprint small.

15. Children

Chef Fresh is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, which can be up to 16 in parts of the EU). If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Automated decision-making

AI scanning suggests item names, quantities, and estimated expiry dates to save you typing, and recipe suggestions propose dishes; you can always review and edit both (see section 5). It does not make any decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you, so it is not "automated decision-making" in the sense of GDPR Article 22.
