Privacy Policy

Chef Fresh · Effective date: July 27, 2026

Chef Fresh ("the app", "we") is a kitchen inventory app that helps you track groceries and reduce food waste. We collect only what the app needs to work: there are no ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

1. Data the app handles

  • Account. Signing in with Google or Apple gives us your name, email address, and a unique account ID. If you continue as a guest, only an anonymous random ID is created — no name, no email.
  • Kitchen data. Your fridge items, shopping list, kitchen membership, and chosen display name. This lives on your device and, when you are signed in with an account, is synced to our cloud database so your kitchen follows you across devices. Guest data stays on the device only. Your consumed/wasted history (the Recap feature) is stored on your device only and is never uploaded.
  • AI scan content. The photo, voice recording, or text you submit for scanning is sent over an encrypted connection through our server to Google's Gemini API, used once to recognize your groceries, and not stored by us afterwards. Please don't include personal information in scans.
  • Subscription status. Payments happen entirely in the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat links your subscription status to your account ID so Premium works across your devices. We never see your payment details.
  • Technical data. A per-install identifier tracks the free-scan trial, and our servers use standard rate limiting that briefly processes IP addresses — both purely to prevent abuse.

2. What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data — ever.

3. Service providers

The app relies on a small set of processors to function: Supabase (accounts and cloud database), Google Gemini (AI scan processing), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Apple / Google (sign-in and payments). They process data only to provide these functions.

4. Shared kitchens

If you share a kitchen, its members see the shared inventory and the display name you chose — that's the point of the feature. Leaving a kitchen keeps a private local copy of the items on your own device.

5. Notifications

Expiry reminders and recap notifications are optional and scheduled locally on your device. Turn them off anytime in the app's Settings or your device's system settings.

6. Deleting your data

You are in control:

  • Delete Account (Settings → Account) permanently deletes your account and the data synced to our servers. This cannot be undone.
  • Signing out removes the synced data from that device.
  • Deleting the app removes all on-device data, including Recap history.

For any other privacy request, contact us at the address below and we'll help as required by the laws that apply to you.

7. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (TLS), and cloud data is protected with row-level security so each account can only reach its own kitchen's data.

8. Children

Chef Fresh is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date; material changes will be communicated in the app.
